# Consent Framework

This is a free consent framework for any and all organizations that may need it. If you, like us, found a need for a framework or document that outlines how you handle matters and transgressions of consent, you are in the right place!

Please note, this framework is still a work in progress and is not finished - in fact, we have only just begun. Thank you for your help along this journey!

---

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is this free?

Yes, this framework is offered free (both as in beer and as in freedom) to all. It is licensed under the [GPL V2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License#Version_2). This is a type of [Copyleft License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copyleft). What this means is that the only requirement for the use of this framework is that you contribute back changes you may make and credit the original source. To do so, please [fork the project,](https://docs.github.com/en/pull-requests/collaborating-with-pull-requests/working-with-forks/fork-a-repo) make your changes, and create a [pull request](https://docs.github.com/en/pull-requests/collaborating-with-pull-requests/proposing-changes-to-your-work-with-pull-requests/creating-a-pull-request). We may or may not incorporate changes you suggest in your PR, but even rejected PRs will still show as breadcrumbs for others who may like changes that you find work better for your group or organization - and possibly theirs too!

## I don't run a group. Can I make suggestions and contributions?

Yes! Even if you do not run a group and do not wish to fork the project or write specific wording, you can also file criticisms or make suggestions by opening an [issue](https://github.com/PrivateAnon/ConsentFramework/issues). Others as well as the maintainers of this project may then comment on or discuss the idea, criticism, or suggestion. Finally, the suggested changes will either be incorporated, or the issue will be closed by the maintainers with a note explaining why we do not feel the topic raised is a good fit to incorporate as a change.

## I don't agree with [thing] but you didn't accept my change!

OK... That means we didn't agree - but again, we invite you to fork the project and alter this framework to your liking. One size does not fit all.

If you would like rationale as to why we opted to incorporate a certain policy, you will typically find an explanation/justification/rationale and the thought process behind any given thing in the commit for that line or section and in the commit log generally. It is also worth looking for related open or closed issues on the "issues" tab.
